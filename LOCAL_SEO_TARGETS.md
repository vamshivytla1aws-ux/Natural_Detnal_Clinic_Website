## 4. "Near Me" Proximity Signal Strategy

Google determines proximity from the user's physical location. We do **not** add "near me" literally across pages. Instead, relevance is established through:

- ✅ Full NAP (Name, Address, Phone) in schema and visible content
- ✅ "Ramachandrapuram, Hyderabad" in title tags and supporting copy
- ✅ Full address with landmark (South India Shopping Mall) visible on homepage and contact
- ✅ Google Maps embed on homepage and contact
- ✅ Mobile Call / WhatsApp / Directions bar
- ✅ Genuine Google reviews with local context
- ✅ LocalFAQs on homepage covering "near me" intent naturally
- ✅ GBP alignment once video verification is complete

## 8. Nearby Area Strategy

No doorway pages for individual nearby localities. Natural sentences cover nearby patient context, such as:

> "Natural Dental Clinic is located in Ramachandrapuram, Hyderabad, and is conveniently accessible for patients from nearby areas including Beeramguda, Ameenpur and Patancheru."

Do not create `/dentist-beeramguda`, `/dentist-ameenpur`, `/dentist-patancheru` etc. unless Search Console data shows legitimate demand and each page would contain genuinely unique, useful information.
